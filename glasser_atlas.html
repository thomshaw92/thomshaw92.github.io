About this Atlas Viewer

This interactive visualisation shows multiple cortical parcellation atlases mapped onto the left hemisphere of the fsaverage template brain. You can select from several popular atlases using the dropdown menu.

Available Atlases:

  • Glasser Atlas (HCP-MMP1.0): 180-region parcellation based on multi-modal MRI data (Glasser et al., 2016)
  • Destrieux Atlas: Anatomically-based parcellation with 74 cortical regions (Destrieux et al., 2010)
  • Yeo 7 Networks: Functional connectivity-based networks derived from resting-state fMRI (Yeo et al., 2011)
  • Yeo 17 Networks: Finer-grained functional networks with 17 subdivisions (Yeo et al., 2011)

Toggle between the folded (pial) surface showing the brain's natural gyri and sulci, and the inflated surface which reveals regions hidden within sulcal folds. Black boundary lines delineate each parcellation region.

Information only: this atlas viewer is provided for informational, educational, and research demonstration purposes only. It is not intended for clinical decision-making, diagnostic use, or commercial use.
