About

Cortical atlases on the left hemisphere of the FreeSurfer fsaverage brain. Pick an atlas from the menu and switch between the folded and inflated surface.

  • Glasser (HCP-MMP1.0): 180 areas from multi-modal MRI (Glasser et al., 2016)
  • Destrieux: 74 to 82 anatomical regions (Destrieux et al., 2010)
  • Desikan-Killiany: 36 anatomical regions (Desikan et al., 2006), and a subdivided version with 225 (Khan et al., 2018)
  • Yeo: 7 or 17 resting-state networks (Yeo et al., 2011)
  • PALS: Brodmann areas, lobes, and others (Van Essen, 2005)
  • OASIS CHUBS
  • Projected from volumes: Harvard-Oxford cortical, HMAT motor areas, and Schaefer 2018 (7 networks)

Information only: for teaching and research demonstration, not for clinical or commercial use.
