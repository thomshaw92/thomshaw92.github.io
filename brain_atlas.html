About this Atlas Viewer

This interactive visualisation shows multiple cortical parcellation atlases mapped onto the left hemisphere of the fsaverage template brain. The atlas selection dropdown allows you to explore different parcellation schemes available through MNE-Python and FreeSurfer.

Available Atlases: The viewer includes all standard atlases available in the MNE-Python distribution, including:

  • Glasser Atlas (HCP-MMP1.0): 180-region parcellation based on multi-modal MRI data (Glasser et al., 2016)
  • Destrieux Atlases: Anatomically-based parcellations with 74-82 cortical regions (Destrieux et al., 2010)
  • Desikan-Killiany Atlas: 36-region anatomical parcellation (Desikan et al., 2006)
  • Yeo Networks: Functional connectivity-based networks (7 or 17 networks) derived from resting-state fMRI (Yeo et al., 2011)
  • PALS Atlases: Various PALS parcellations including Brodmann areas and lobe divisions (Van Essen, 2005)
  • OASIS CHUBS: OASIS CHUBS parcellation
  • And more... All atlases available in your MNE installation

Custom Atlases: The build system also supports downloading and processing custom atlas annotation files from URLs. Use the command-line tool to add new atlases to the collection.

Toggle between the folded (pial) surface showing the brain's natural gyri and sulci, and the inflated surface which reveals regions hidden within sulcal folds. Black boundary lines delineate each parcellation region.

Information only: this atlas viewer is provided for informational, educational, and research demonstration purposes only. It is not intended for clinical decision-making, diagnostic use, or commercial use.
