About

Dr Shaw is a neuroscientist and MRI researcher at The University of Queensland. He develops imaging biomarkers for Motor Neuron Disease (MND), using 7T and 3T MRI and MR spectroscopy of the brain and spinal cord.

He helped establish the Asia-Pacific MND Imaging Initiative (AMII), which harmonises MRI protocols across Australian centres. His work is funded by FightMND, NHMRC, and MND Research Australia, and he takes part in weekly clinics at the Royal Brisbane & Women's Hospital.
